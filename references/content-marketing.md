# Content Marketing & Influencer Strategy

## Content Marketing Framework

### Content Pillars

Define 3-5 content pillars that align with your product and audience:

| Pillar | Example (for a kitchen gadget brand) | Content Types |
|--------|--------------------------------------|---------------|
| **Education/How-to** | "How to clean stainless steel", "5 knife skills" | Blog, YouTube, TikTok, carousel |
| **Inspiration/Ideas** | "10 weeknight meals under 30 min", "kitchen organization" | Pinterest, Instagram, blog roundups |
| **Product/Brand** | Product demos, behind-the-scenes, founder story | TikTok, Reels, email, about page |
| **Social Proof** | Customer reviews, UGC, before/after, testimonials | Stories, TikTok, product pages, email |
| **Entertainment** | Relatable humor, trends, day-in-the-life | TikTok, Reels, Shorts |

### Content Funnel Mapping

| Funnel Stage | Content Goal | Content Types | CTA |
|-------------|-------------|---------------|-----|
| **Awareness** | Reach new audiences, solve top-of-mind problems | How-to guides, listicles, trending videos, infographics | Follow, save, share, visit blog |
| **Consideration** | Build trust, show product as solution | Product reviews, comparisons, tutorials, case studies | Join email, download guide, watch demo |
| **Decision** | Remove purchase barriers, drive action | Detailed specs, FAQs, testimonials, limited offers, demos | Buy now, add to cart, use discount |
| **Retention** | Increase LTV, build loyalty | Usage tips, recipes/ideas, exclusive content, community | Repurchase, refer, join loyalty, UGC |

### SEO Content Strategy

#### Keyword-Driven Content Planning
1. **Map keywords to content types**:
   - Informational → blog posts, guides, tutorials
   - Commercial → comparison pages, "best of" lists, reviews
   - Transactional → product pages, landing pages, category pages
2. **Create topic clusters**: Pillar page (broad) + cluster pages (specific)
3. **Internal linking**: Every blog post links to relevant product pages
4. **Update old content**: Refresh outdated posts (Google favors fresh content)

## Influencer / KOL Marketing

### Influencer Tier Strategy

| Tier | Followers | Engagement Rate | Cost | Best For |
|------|-----------|----------------|------|----------|
| **Nano** | 1K-10K | 5-10%+ | Free product + small fee ($50-200) | Hyper-niche trust, UGC, high conversion |
| **Micro** | 10K-100K | 3-8% | $200-1K per post | Niche reach, balanced cost/engagement |
| **Mid** | 100K-500K | 2-5% | $1K-5K per post | Broader reach, brand awareness |
| **Macro** | 500K-1M | 1-3% | $5K-20K per post | Mass awareness, brand credibility |
| **Mega/Celebrity** | 1M+ | 0.5-2% | $20K+ per post | Major launches, brand prestige |

#### Recommendation for Most Ecommerce Brands
- **70% micro/nano creators** (high ROI, authentic, lots of UGC)
- **20% mid creators** (reach + credibility)
- **10% macro/mega** (brand awareness, social proof)

### Finding Influencers

#### Platform-Specific Discovery
- **TikTok**: Creator Marketplace, search hashtags, look at who comments on your posts, competitors' tagged creators
- **Instagram**: Search niche hashtags, look at tagged posts on competitor accounts, Creator Marketplace, Collabs feature
- **YouTube**: Search product review videos in your niche, look at related channels
- **Pinterest**: Search niche topics, look at top pinners in your category
- **Bloggers**: Google "[niche] blog", look at top ranking blogs, reach out for sponsored posts/reviews

#### Criteria for Selecting Influencers
- [ ] **Relevance**: Audience matches your target customer (demographics, interests)
- [ ] **Engagement rate**: >3% for micro, >2% for mid (calculate: (likes+comments)/followers)
- [ ] **Content quality**: Aesthetic matches your brand, video/photo quality acceptable
- [ ] **Authenticity**: Real comments (not bot/engagement pods), genuine tone
- [ ] **Brand safety**: No controversial content, no competitor promotions recently
- [ ] **Past performance**: Have they promoted similar products? How did those perform?
- [ ] **Audience location**: Matches your target market (check analytics if available)

### Outreach Strategy

#### Cold Outreach Template
```
Subject: Collaboration opportunity — [Your Brand] × [Creator Name]

Hi [Creator Name],

I've been following your content on [platform] and love your [specific post/series/style].
Your [specific content example] really stood out — [genuine compliment].

I'm reaching out from [Your Brand], we make [one-line product description].
We think your audience would love [product] because [specific reason tied to their content].

We'd love to collaborate with you on:
- [Option 1: e.g., A dedicated TikTok/Reel reviewing our product]
- [Option 2: e.g., An honest unboxing and demo]
- [Option 3: e.g., A discount code for your followers]

We can offer:
- [Compensation: free product + $X fee, or affiliate commission, or both]
- [Additional: unique discount code for your audience, custom link for tracking]
- [Extras: repost on our channels, feature in our email, long-term partnership potential]

Would you be open to discussing this? I'd love to hear your thoughts and see if
there's a good fit.

Best,
[Your Name]
[Your Brand]
[Contact info]
```

#### Outreach Best Practices
- **Personalize**: Reference a specific post, not generic "love your content"
- **Keep it concise**: Creators get many DMs/emails — get to the point
- **Offer value first**: What's in it for them? (money, product, audience value)
- **Multiple channels**: DM on platform + email (if available in bio)
- **Follow up**: 1 follow-up after 3-5 days (not more — respect their time)
- **Track response rate**: Aim for 15-30% response rate (adjust targeting if lower)

### Collaboration Models

| Model | Description | Best For |
|-------|-------------|----------|
| **Paid post** | Flat fee for dedicated content | Established creators, guaranteed output |
| **Product for post** | Free product in exchange for content | Nano/micro creators, low budget |
| **Affiliate** | Commission per sale (10-30%) | Performance-focused, low upfront cost |
| **Paid + affiliate** | Lower fee + commission | Motivated creators, long-term |
| **Brand ambassador** | Ongoing monthly retainer + products | Top performers, brand consistency |
| **Whitelisting** | Run ads from creator's account (Spark Ads) | Scaling winning creative |
| **UGC license** | Pay for content rights, use in your ads | Ad creative supply, no posting required |

### Campaign Tracking & ROI

#### Tracking Methods
- **Unique discount codes**: `CREATORNAME15` — track attributed sales
- **Unique links/UTM**: `?utm_source=tiktok&utm_medium=influencer&utm_campaign=creatorname`
- **Affiliate platform**: ShareASale, Impact, Refersion — automatic tracking
- **Landing pages**: Unique landing page per creator (e.g., `brand.com/creatorname`)
- **Promo period**: Track sales during + 7 days after post (attribution window)

#### ROI Calculation
```
Influencer ROI = (Revenue attributed - Total cost) / Total cost × 100

Total cost = Fee + Product cost + Shipping + Any extras

Example:
- Fee: $300
- Product cost: $25
- Revenue attributed (via code): $1,500
- ROI = ($1,500 - $325) / $325 = 361% (4.6x ROAS)
```

#### Benchmarks
- **Good influencer ROAS**: 3-5x (for product sales)
- **Great influencer ROAS**: 5x+
- **Break-even ROAS**: 1 / Gross margin % (e.g., 60% margin → 1.67x break-even)
- **UGC content value**: Even if direct sales are low, content can be reused in ads (calculate ad creative value)

### Long-Term Influencer Program

1. **Identify top performers**: After 3 months, identify creators with highest ROAS
2. **Offer ambassador deals**: Monthly retainer + exclusive products + early access
3. **Build a creator community**: Private group, shared resources, first looks
4. **Whitelist their best content**: Run Spark Ads / whitelisted ads from their accounts
5. **Co-create products**: Top creators can help design or name products
6. **Annual contracts**: Lock in top creators for 6-12 month partnerships
