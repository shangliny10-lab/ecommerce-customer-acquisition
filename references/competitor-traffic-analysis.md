# Competitor Traffic Analysis

## Analysis Framework

### What to Analyze

For each competitor, collect data across these dimensions:

#### 1. Traffic Volume & Source Breakdown
- **Estimated monthly visits**: Use ranges (e.g., 50K-100K/mo) unless exact data available
- **Traffic source mix**: Organic search %, Paid search %, Social %, Direct %, Referral %, Email %
- **Traffic trend**: Growing, stable, or declining? (based on visible signals like content frequency, ad activity, social growth)
- **Geographic distribution**: Which countries drive most traffic

#### 2. Organic Search Strategy
- **Top ranking keywords**: Which keywords bring them the most organic traffic?
- **Keyword count**: How many keywords do they rank for? (indicates content breadth)
- **Content strategy**: Blog? Guides? Comparison pages? Video transcripts?
- **Domain authority / backlink profile**: How authoritative is their site?
- **SERP features**: Do they own featured snippets, image packs, video carousels?

#### 3. Paid Advertising Strategy
- **Ad platforms**: Google Ads? Meta? TikTok? Amazon PPC?
- **Ad spend estimate**: Visible ad frequency and reach → estimate monthly spend range
- **Ad creative patterns**: What messaging angles? What visuals? What CTAs?
- **Landing pages**: Where do ads send traffic? Product page? Landing page? Collection?
- **Offer strategy**: Discounts? Free shipping? BOGO? Limited-time?
- **Keyword targeting**: Which keywords are they bidding on? (visible via ad appearance)

#### 4. Social Media Presence
- **Platform presence**: Which platforms? Follower counts?
- **Posting frequency**: How often? What times?
- **Content mix**: Product shots? Lifestyle? UGC? Tutorials? Behind-the-scenes?
- **Engagement rate**: Likes/comments/shares per post relative to follower count
- **Top-performing content**: Which posts get the most engagement?
- **Hashtag strategy**: Branded? Trending? Niche?
- **Community management**: Do they respond to comments? Run groups?

#### 5. Influencer / KOL Strategy
- **Influencer types**: Macro? Micro? Nano? What niches?
- **Collaboration frequency**: How many creators per month?
- **Content format**: Dedicated videos? Mentions? Hauls? Reviews?
- **Compensation model**: Paid? Free product? Affiliate?
- **Platform focus**: TikTok? Instagram? YouTube?
- **Brand ambassador program**: Do they have ongoing ambassadors?

#### 6. Email & Retention
- **Email capture**: Pop-ups? Lead magnets? Discount for signup?
- **Email frequency**: How often do they send?
- **Email content**: Promotional only? Educational? Story-driven?
- **Loyalty/referral**: Do they have a program? What incentives?
- **Post-purchase flow**: Review requests? Upsell? Community?

#### 7. Conversion & UX
- **Landing page structure**: Above-the-fold content? Social proof? Urgency?
- **Product page quality**: Images? Videos? Reviews? Q&A? Size charts?
- **Checkout flow**: Guest checkout? Multiple payment options? One-page?
- **Trust signals**: Badges? Guarantees? Testimonials? Press mentions?
- **Exit intent**: Pop-ups? Special offers?

### Content Gaps
- **Keywords competitors rank for that we don't**: High-value targets
- **Content types competitors use that we don't**: e.g., video tutorials, comparison guides
- **Topics competitors cover that we miss**: Use their blog/sitemap as a topic map
- **Content depth gaps**: Where competitors have thin content, we can out-depth them

### Channel Gaps
- **Platforms competitors ignore**: Opportunity to own an under-served channel
- **Platforms competitors dominate**: Need differentiation or alternative approach
- **Audience segments competitors miss**: e.g., competitors target 18-24, we can target 35-45

### Messaging Gaps
- **Benefits competitors emphasize**: What's their unique selling proposition?
- **Benefits competitors ignore**: Can we own an unclaimed benefit?
- **Pain points competitors don't address**: Opportunity to be the solution
- **Tone and style**: Do they all sound the same? Can we differentiate with voice?

## Reverse-Engineering Playbook

### If Competitor Dominates Organic Search
1. Analyze their top 20 ranking pages
2. Identify content gaps (topics they don't cover or cover thinly)
3. Create better, more comprehensive content on those topics
4. Build backlinks through outreach, guest posts, digital PR
5. Target long-tail keywords they overlook (they can't cover everything)
6. Optimize for SERP features (featured snippets, FAQs, video)

### If Competitor Dominates Paid Ads
1. Study their ad creative and messaging (what angles do they use?)
2. Identify their weaknesses (poor landing pages? weak offers?)
3. Differentiate with unique creative angles they don't use
4. Target keywords/audiences they overlook (niche long-tail, specific demographics)
5. Optimize landing page conversion rate to outperform their ROAS
6. Use retargeting to capture users who visited competitor sites

### If Competitor Dominates Social Media
1. Analyze their top-performing content (what gets engagement?)
2. Identify content formats they don't use (e.g., they do photos, we do video)
3. Partner with micro-influencers in niches they ignore
4. Build community (groups, comment engagement) rather than just broadcasting
5. Leverage trending formats/audio faster than they can
6. Target a sub-audience they don't serve well

### If Competitor Has Weaknesses to Exploit
- **Poor reviews**: Emphasize our quality, offer guarantees, collect reviews aggressively
- **Slow shipping**: Highlight our fast/Free shipping
- **Limited product range**: Cross-sell, bundle, offer one-stop-shop convenience
- **Bad customer service**: Promote our support, easy returns, responsive team
- **Outdated website/UX**: Modern site, mobile optimization, fast checkout
- **No community**: Build brand community, user groups, UGC programs
