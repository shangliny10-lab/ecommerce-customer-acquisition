# Keyword Research Methodology

## Keyword Taxonomy

### By Search Intent

| Intent Type | Definition | Example | Funnel Stage |
|------------|-----------|---------|-------------|
| **Informational** | Seeking knowledge, answers, how-to | "how to clean stainless steel pans" | Awareness |
| **Navigational** | Looking for a specific brand/site | "nike running shoes official" | Consideration |
| **Commercial Investigation** | Researching products/brands before buying | "best wireless earbuds under $100" | Consideration |
| **Transactional** | Ready to purchase | "buy airpods pro 2" | Decision |

### By Keyword Structure

#### 1. Brand Keywords
- Brand name + product: "brandname wireless charger"
- Brand name + modifier: "brandname coupon", "brandname reviews"
- Brand misspellings and variations
- **Strategy**: Defend brand SERP, high conversion, low CPC (if no competitors bidding)

#### 2. Category / Generic Keywords
- Product category: "wireless earbuds", "yoga mats"
- Category + attribute: "wireless earbuds noise cancelling"
- **Strategy**: High volume, high competition, mid-funnel, need strong content and listing

#### 3. Product-Specific Keywords
- Exact product name/model: "airpods pro 2nd generation"
- Product + use case: "airpods pro for running"
- **Strategy**: Lower volume but high intent, good for long-tail and retargeting

#### 4. Problem / Solution Keywords
- "how to fix [problem]", "best way to [solution]"
- "[problem] solution", "[pain point] product"
- **Strategy**: Top-of-funnel content marketing, build brand authority, capture early researchers

#### 5. Comparison Keywords
- "X vs Y", "X alternative", "X vs Y which is better"
- "best [category] for [use case]"
- **Strategy**: Mid-funnel, high commercial intent, comparison content converts well

#### 6. Long-Tail Question Keywords
- "what are the best [category] for [specific person/use]"
- "can I use [product] for [unusual use case]"
- "where to buy [specific product] in [country]"
- **Strategy**: Low competition, high specificity, excellent for content and voice search

#### 7. Local / Regional Keywords
- "[product] [country]", "[category] near me"
- "[product] free shipping [country]"
- **Strategy**: For local markets, add country/language modifiers

## Keyword Research Process

### Step 1: Seed Keyword Generation
Start with these sources:
- Product name, category, and obvious synonyms
- User pain points and problems the product solves
- Use cases and occasions
- Target audience descriptors
- Competitor brand names (for comparison targeting)
- Seasonal and event-related terms

### Step 2: Expand Using Search Suggestions
- Google Autocomplete (type seed keyword, see suggestions)
- "People also ask" boxes
- Related searches at bottom of SERP
- Amazon search bar suggestions
- YouTube search suggestions
- Reddit search (find what people actually ask)
- Forum and Q&A sites (Quora, Stack Exchange)

### Step 3: Analyze Competitor Keywords
- Competitor product titles and bullet points (extract keywords)
- Competitor blog content and article titles
- Competitor meta titles and descriptions (view page source)
- Competitor YouTube video titles and tags
- Competitor social media hashtags and captions
- Tools: SEMrush, Ahrefs, SimilarWeb (if available), or manual SERP analysis

### Step 4: Evaluate and Prioritize
Score each keyword on:

| Factor | What to Assess | Scoring |
|--------|---------------|---------|
| **Search Volume** | How many searches/month? | High/Med/Low (use ranges, not exact numbers unless verified) |
| **Intent** | Is it commercial/transactional? | High/Med/Low purchase intent |
| **Difficulty** | How hard to rank? | Based on SERP competition, domain authority of ranking sites |
| **Relevance** | Does it match our product? | 1-5 scale, must be 4+ to target |
| **Value** | What's the potential revenue? | AOV × conversion likelihood × volume |

### Step 5: Group into Clusters
Organize keywords into topic clusters:
- **Pillar page**: Broad topic (e.g., "wireless earbuds guide")
- **Cluster pages**: Specific subtopics (e.g., "best for running", "noise cancelling comparison", "under $100")
- **Internal linking**: Cluster pages link to pillar, pillar links to clusters

## Marketplace-Specific Keyword Strategy

### Amazon
- **Frontend keywords**: Title, bullet points, description, A+ content
- **Backend keywords**: Search terms field (249 bytes, no repeats, no brand names)
- **Subject matter**: Other fields (style name, intended use, target audience)
- **Keyword placement priority**: Title > bullets > backend > description
- **Character limits**: Title 200 chars (category-dependent), bullets 500 chars each
- **Avoid**: Keyword stuffing, competitor brand names, subjective claims, punctuation in backend

### Google Shopping / Paid Search
- **Product feed keywords**: Title and description in Merchant Center feed
- **Negative keywords**: Critical to avoid wasted spend (e.g., "free", "jobs", "used")
- **Match types**: Exact (high control), Phrase (balanced), Broad (reach, needs negatives)
- **Keyword grouping**: Theme-based ad groups with tight keyword relevance

### TikTok / Social Search
- **Hashtag keywords**: #productcategory, #problem, #solution, #use case
- **Caption keywords**: Natural language, conversational, include product and benefit
- **Trending audio + keyword**: Combine trending sounds with keyword-rich captions
- **Search optimization**: TikTok search is growing — optimize captions and hashtags for discoverability

## Keyword Mapping to Content

| Keyword Type | Content Format | Platform | CTA |
|-------------|---------------|----------|-----|
| Informational / problem-solution | Blog article, how-to guide, YouTube tutorial | Blog, YouTube, Pinterest | "Learn more", email capture, soft product mention |
| Commercial / comparison | Comparison article, "best of" list, review video | Blog, YouTube, Reddit | Product recommendation with affiliate/shop link |
| Transactional | Product page, landing page, shopping ad | Amazon, Shopify, Google Shopping | "Buy now", "Add to cart", limited offer |
| Brand | Brand landing page, about page, storefront | Website, Amazon store | Shop full collection, sign up |
| Long-tail question | FAQ section, Q&A content, Reddit answer | Website FAQ, Quora, Reddit | Link to relevant product page |

## Negative Keyword List (for paid search)

Always consider excluding:
- `free`, `cheap` (if premium product), `jobs`, `career`, `hiring`
- `used`, `secondhand`, `refurbished` (if selling new)
- `diy`, `homemade`, `recipe`, `how to make` (if selling finished product)
- `review` (unless targeting review intent)
- `wholesale`, `bulk`, `distributor` (if B2C only)
- Brand names of competitors (unless doing comparison campaigns)
- Irrelevant contexts (e.g., for "apple" product, exclude "fruit", "recipe", "cider")
