# Social Media Acquisition Playbook

## Platform-Specific Strategies

### TikTok

#### Why It Works for Ecommerce
- Highest organic reach of any platform (algorithm favors new creators)
- Short-form video drives product discovery and impulse purchases
- TikTok Shop integrates shopping natively (US, UK, SEA, LATAM)
- Strong for problem/solution products, visual demos, transformations

#### Content Strategy
| Content Type | Description | Conversion Goal |
|-------------|-------------|----------------|
| **Product demo** | Show product in action, solve a visible problem | Direct purchase |
| **Before/after** | Transformation content (cleaning, beauty, fitness, home) | High impulse |
| **Problem → solution** | Start with relatable pain point, reveal product as fix | Consideration → purchase |
| **Unboxing / ASMR** | Satisfying unpacking, product reveal | Awareness, desire |
| **Day in the life** | Integrate product naturally into daily routine | Trust, relatability |
| **My honest review** | Authentic-sounding review (even from brand account) | Trust, decision |
| **Trend participation** | Use trending audio/format with product twist | Reach, virality |
| **Comment bait** | Ask questions, create debate, respond to comments | Engagement, algorithm boost |

#### Posting Cadence
- **Minimum**: 3-5 posts/week (algorithm favors consistency)
- **Optimal**: 1-2 posts/day for growth phase
- **Best times**: Test, but generally 6-10 AM and 7-11 PM local time
- **Duration**: 15-30 seconds for highest completion rate (up to 60s for tutorials)

#### Hashtag Strategy
- 3-5 hashtags per post (not 20+ — looks spammy)
- Mix: 1 branded + 1-2 niche (100K-1M views) + 1-2 broad/trending
- Example: `#brandname` + `#kitchengadgets` + `#cleaninghacks` + `#tiktokmademebuyit`
- Create a branded hashtag for UGC collection

#### TikTok Shop Integration
- Shoppable videos: Tag products directly in video
- Live shopping: Regular live streams with demos and exclusive deals
- Affiliate: Let creators earn commission by promoting your products
- Shop tab: Curated storefront within TikTok

#### Growth Tactics
- **Duet/Stitch**: React to viral videos with your product twist
- **Comment on big accounts**: Leave helpful/funny comments on viral videos (profile traffic)
- **Series**: Create multi-part series (e.g., "Day 1-7 using product")
- **Spark Ads**: Boost your best organic posts as ads (proven creative = lower CPA)

### Instagram

#### Content Strategy by Format
| Format | Best For | Posting Frequency |
|--------|---------|------------------|
| **Reels** | Reach, discovery, product demos, trends | 3-5/week |
| **Feed Posts** | Brand aesthetic, product launches, high-quality imagery | 3-4/week |
| **Stories** | Engagement, behind-the-scenes, polls, Q&A, time-sensitive offers | Daily (5-10 frames) |
| **Carousels** | Education, tutorials, before/after, multiple products | 1-2/week |
| **Live** | Q&A, product launches, tutorials, influencer collabs | 1-2/month |

#### Reels Strategy
- Hook in first 1-2 seconds (text overlay + visual)
- 15-30 seconds optimal
- Use trending audio (check Reels tab for what's rising)
- Captions: 3-5 lines, include keywords and CTA
- Cover image: On-brand, text overlay for clarity in grid

#### Shopping Features
- **Shoppable posts**: Tag products in feed posts and Reels
- **Shop tab**: Instagram Storefront
- **Product stickers**: In Stories, link directly to product
- **Collections**: Curate products into themed collections
- **Live Shopping**: Tag products during live streams

#### Story Tactics
- Polls and quizzes (boost engagement, algorithm signal)
- Q&A stickers (collect content ideas, build community)
- Countdown stickers (for launches, sales)
- Link stickers (drive traffic, only for 10K+ followers or verified)
- Behind-the-scenes (humanize brand)
- User-generated content reposts
- Limited-time offers (create urgency)

#### Growth Tactics
- Collaborate with micro-influencers (takeovers, Reels collabs)
- Use relevant hashtags (mix of niche and broad, 5-10 per post)
- Engage authentically in comments (respond within 1 hour)
- Share user content (tag creators, build loyalty)
- Run giveaways (follow + tag + share for entry)

### Pinterest

#### Why It Works for Ecommerce
- **Search-driven**, not social — users come to find and plan purchases
- Long content lifespan (pins can drive traffic for months/years)
- Strong for visual products: home decor, fashion, beauty, food, DIY, wedding
- High purchase intent (users are in planning/buying mode)
- Excellent for driving traffic to blog content and product pages

#### Pin Strategy
| Pin Type | Description | Best For |
|----------|-------------|----------|
| **Product pins** | Clean product image, price, direct link | Direct purchase |
| **Idea pins** | Multi-page video/story pins (5-10 pages) | Engagement, followers |
| **Blog/content pins** | Link to articles, guides, tutorials | Traffic, email capture |
| **Inspiration pins** | Lifestyle imagery, mood boards | Brand awareness, desire |
| **Video pins** | Short video (15s-1min) | Reach, demos |

#### Best Practices
- **Pin size**: 2:3 vertical (1000×1500px) — performs best
- **Design**: Clean, high-contrast, text overlay (benefit-driven headline)
- **Pin frequency**: 5-15 pins/day (use scheduler like Tailwind)
- **Boards**: Create themed boards (e.g., "Kitchen Organization Ideas", "Minimalist Home Decor")
- **Keywords**: Pin titles and descriptions should be keyword-rich (Pinterest is a search engine)
- **Rich pins**: Enable product pins with real-time pricing and availability

#### Growth Tactics
- Pin consistently (daily, not weekly binges)
- Create fresh pins for the same content (different angles/designs)
- Join group boards in your niche
- Follow and repin from top accounts in your niche
- Optimize pin titles for search (what would users type to find this?)
- Link pins to relevant landing pages (not just homepage)

### YouTube / YouTube Shorts

#### Long-Form YouTube
- **Best for**: Product reviews, tutorials, comparisons, brand storytelling
- **Video length**: 8-15 minutes for tutorials/reviews (enough for ad revenue and depth)
- **SEO**: Title, description, tags, thumbnails — YouTube is the 2nd largest search engine
- **Conversion**: Links in description, cards, end screens, pinned comment
- **Cadence**: 1-2 videos/week (quality over quantity)

#### YouTube Shorts
- **Best for**: Quick demos, tips, clips from long-form, trends
- **Length**: Under 60 seconds, vertical
- **Reach**: High organic reach (YouTube is pushing Shorts)
- **Conversion**: Link in description (Shorts don't have clickable links in-video yet)
- **Cadence**: 3-5/week

### Facebook

#### Strategy for Ecommerce
- **Groups**: Create or participate in niche groups (high trust, targeted)
- **Marketplace**: List products (free traffic, local buyers)
- **Reels**: Facebook Reels (reuse TikTok content)
- **Pages**: Brand page with posts, reviews, shop tab
- **Events**: Virtual launches, live shopping events
- **Messenger**: Chatbots for customer service, abandoned cart reminders

#### Best Practices
- Video content outperforms static posts
- Post when audience is active (check Insights)
- Use Facebook Shop for native checkout
- Run contests and giveaways (follow rules)
- Leverage customer reviews and testimonials

### Reddit

#### Strategy (High Trust, Low Self-Promotion)
- **Find subreddits**: r/[productcategory], r/[problem], r/[hobby], r/BuyItForLife, r/Frugal
- **Participate first**: Answer questions, give advice, build karma before any promotion
- **Content types**:
  - Helpful answers mentioning your product (when genuinely relevant)
  - "I made X" posts (if you're the maker, be transparent)
  - Detailed comparisons (include your product as one option)
  - Case studies / results (with data)
- **Rules**: Read subreddit rules carefully, many ban self-promotion
- **Never**: Spam links, use throwaway accounts for promotion, astroturf
- **Best for**: Niche hobby products, tech gadgets, problem/solution products

## Cross-Platform Content Repurposing

### Content Atomization
One core piece of content → multiple formats across platforms:

| Core Content | TikTok | Instagram | Pinterest | YouTube | Email |
|-------------|--------|-----------|-----------|---------|-------|
| Product tutorial video | 15s clip (hook + result) | Reel (30s) + carousel (steps) | Idea pin (step-by-step) | Full tutorial (10min) | "How to use" email |
| Blog article | Key takeaway as text-on-video | Carousel (5 key points) | Pin (title + image) | Video summary | Article digest |
| Customer review | "Real customer says" video | Story + feed post | Pin (quote graphic) | Compilation video | Social proof email |
| Product launch | Demo + reveal Reel | Feed post + Reels + Stories | Multiple pins (different angles) | Launch video | Launch email sequence |
