# Traffic Channel Map

## Channel Taxonomy

### 1. Organic Search (SEO)
- **Marketplace search**: Amazon A9/A10, TEMU search, Shopee search, eBay Cassini
- **Web search**: Google, Bing, Baidu, Yandex, Naver
- **App store search**: iOS App Store, Google Play (for DTC apps)
- **Key metrics**: organic ranking, impression share, click-through rate, organic sessions

### 2. Paid Search / PPC
- **Marketplace PPC**: Amazon Sponsored Products/Brands/Display, TEMU Ads, Shopee Ads, Walmart Sponsored Products
- **Search engine ads**: Google Ads (Search), Bing Ads, Naver Search Ads, Baidu SEM
- **Key metrics**: CPC, CTR, ACOS/TACOS, ROAS, conversion rate, cost per acquisition

### 3. Paid Social
- **Meta**: Facebook Ads, Instagram Ads (Feed, Stories, Reels, Explore)
- **TikTok**: TikTok Ads (In-Feed, TopView, Spark Ads, Branded Effects)
- **Pinterest**: Promoted Pins, Shopping Ads
- **Snapchat**: Snap Ads, Collection Ads
- **Reddit**: Promoted Posts, Conversation Ads
- **Key metrics**: CPM, CPC, CTR, video view rate, CPL, ROAS, frequency

### 4. Organic Social
- **Short-form video**: TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, Facebook Reels
- **Visual discovery**: Pinterest, Instagram Feed, Xiaohongshu/RED (China)
- **Community/forums**: Reddit, Facebook Groups, Discord, Quora
- **Video/long-form**: YouTube, Bilibili (China)
- **Key metrics**: reach, engagement rate, follower growth, profile visits, click-through to store

### 5. Influencer / KOL Marketing
- **Macro influencers**: 100K+ followers, brand awareness focus
- **Micro influencers**: 10K-100K followers, higher engagement, niche authority
- **Nano influencers**: 1K-10K followers, ultra-high trust, low cost
- **Affiliate creators**: Performance-based, pay per sale
- **Platforms**: TikTok Creator Marketplace, Instagram Collabs, YouTube BrandConnect, AspireIQ, Upfluence
- **Key metrics**: engagement rate, reach, CPE (cost per engagement), affiliate revenue, attribution

### 6. Content Marketing
- **Blog/SEO articles**: Targeting informational and commercial keywords
- **Video content**: YouTube tutorials, product reviews, how-to guides
- **Podcast**: Sponsorships, guest appearances
- **Guest posting**: Backlinks and referral traffic
- **Lead magnets**: Ebooks, checklists, templates (email capture)
- **Key metrics**: organic traffic, backlinks, time on page, email signups, assisted conversions

### 7. Email Marketing
- **Welcome series**: Onboarding new subscribers
- **Abandoned cart**: Recovery for checkout drop-offs
- **Browse abandonment**: Re-engage product viewers
- **Post-purchase**: Upsell, cross-sell, review requests
- **Win-back**: Re-engage lapsed customers
- **Promotional**: Sales, launches, seasonal campaigns
- **Key metrics**: open rate, click rate, conversion rate, revenue per email, unsubscribe rate

### 8. Retargeting / Remarketing
- **Site retargeting**: Ads to past website visitors
- **Cart retargeting**: Ads to cart abandoners (dynamic product ads)
- **Customer list retargeting**: Upload email/phone lists to ad platforms
- **Lookalike audiences**: Based on existing customers
- **Key metrics**: frequency cap, CTR, conversion rate, ROAS, cost per re-engagement

### 9. Referral / Affiliate
- **Referral program**: Reward existing customers for referring friends
- **Affiliate network**: ShareASale, CJ Affiliate, Impact, Amazon Associates
- **Coupon/deal sites**: RetailMeNot, Slickdeals, DealNews
- **Cashback sites**: Rakuten, TopCashback
- **Key metrics**: referral rate, affiliate revenue share, new customer percentage, LTV of referred customers

### 10. Marketplaces & Aggregators
- **Comparison engines**: Google Shopping, PriceGrabber, Nextag
- **Product discovery**: Pinterest Shopping, Instagram Shopping, TikTok Shop
- **Daily deals**: Groupon, LivingSocial (selective use)
- **Key metrics**: listing impressions, CTR, conversion, margin after fees

### 11. PR & Brand Mentions
- **Press releases**: Product launches, milestones
- **Media outreach**: Bloggers, journalists, publications
- **Award submissions**: Industry awards, "best of" lists
- **Expert contributions**: HARO, SourceBottle, quoted in articles
- **Key metrics**: mentions, backlinks, referral traffic, brand search volume

### 12. Community & Events
- **Online communities**: Niche forums, Facebook Groups, Reddit, Discord
- **Webinars**: Educational, product demos
- **Virtual events**: Launches, Q&A sessions
- **Offline events**: Pop-ups, trade shows (if applicable)
- **Key metrics**: attendance, engagement, leads generated, community growth

## Channel Selection Framework

### By Funnel Stage
| Stage | Primary Channels | Secondary Channels |
|-------|-----------------|-------------------|
| Awareness | Paid social, organic social, KOL, PR, content | Display ads, YouTube |
| Consideration | SEO, content, retargeting, email, comparison | KOL reviews, YouTube |
| Decision | PPC/search, email, retargeting, affiliate | Marketplaces, reviews |
| Retention | Email, loyalty, referral, organic social | Community, SMS |

### By Budget Level
| Budget | Focus Channels | Rationale |
|--------|---------------|-----------|
| <$500/mo | Organic social, SEO content, email, micro-KOL barter | Low cost, high effort, compounding returns |
| $500-$2K/mo | + Paid social (small tests), marketplace PPC, affiliate | Validate paid channels with controlled spend |
| $2K-$10K/mo | + Scale winning paid, KOL partnerships, PR, retargeting | Multi-channel, optimize for efficiency |
| >$10K/mo | + Full funnel, brand campaigns, agency support, events | Scale what works, invest in brand equity |

### By Product Type
| Product Type | Best Channels | Notes |
|-------------|--------------|-------|
| Visual/aspirational (fashion, beauty, home) | Instagram, Pinterest, TikTok, KOL | Visual platforms drive discovery |
| Problem/solution (gadgets, tools, health) | YouTube reviews, SEO content, Reddit, Google Ads | Demonstration and education convert |
| Low-ticket impulse (<$30) | TikTok, Facebook impulse ads, deal sites | Fast decision, emotional trigger |
| High-ticket consideration (>$100) | SEO, email nurture, retargeting, KOL reviews, comparison | Longer decision cycle needs trust building |
| B2B / wholesale | LinkedIn, content, email outreach, webinars, trade shows | Relationship-driven, longer sales cycle |
