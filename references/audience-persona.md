# Audience Persona Building

## Persona Framework

### Core Dimensions

Every ecommerce audience persona must cover these dimensions:

#### 1. Demographic
- Age range (5-year bands, e.g., 25-30)
- Gender identity (if relevant to product)
- Location (country, region, urban/suburban/rural)
- Income / household income range
- Education level
- Occupation / industry
- Life stage (student, young professional, new parent, empty nester, retiree)

#### 2. Psychographic
- Values and beliefs (e.g., sustainability-conscious, price-sensitive, brand-loyal)
- Lifestyle (active, homebody, frequent traveler, fitness-focused)
- Interests and hobbies
- Personality traits (early adopter, cautious researcher, impulse buyer)
- Risk tolerance (willing to try new brands vs. sticks to known brands)
- Tech savviness level

#### 3. Behavioral
- Online shopping frequency
- Average order value range
- Preferred device (mobile vs. desktop)
- Preferred shopping times (weeknights, weekends, lunch breaks)
- Research habits (reads reviews, compares prices, watches videos)
- Brand loyalty level
- Return behavior frequency
- Subscription/loyalty program participation

#### 4. Pain Points & Motivations
- **Pain points**: What problems are they trying to solve? What frustrates them about current solutions?
- **Motivations**: What drives the purchase? (convenience, status, health, savings, gift, self-care)
- **Purchase triggers**: What event or moment sparks the buying decision? (season change, life event, ad exposure, friend recommendation, pain point reaching threshold)
- **Purchase barriers**: What stops them from buying? (price, trust, shipping time, return policy, unfamiliar brand, sizing uncertainty)

#### 5. Media Consumption
- **Social platforms**: Which platforms, how often, what content type they consume
- **Search behavior**: What they search for, search engine preference
- **Content formats**: Video, articles, podcasts, reviews, tutorials
- **Influencer types**: Who they follow and trust (micro vs. macro, niche experts vs. general lifestyle)
- **Email behavior**: Opens promotional emails? Uses coupons?
- **Ad sensitivity**: Ad-blind? Responds to which ad formats?

#### 6. Purchase Journey
- **Awareness**: How do they first discover products like yours? (social, search, friend, ad, store)
- **Consideration**: How do they evaluate options? (reviews, price comparison, video demos, trying in-store)
- **Decision**: What closes the sale? (discount, free shipping, social proof, urgency, brand trust)
- **Post-purchase**: How do they feel? Do they share? Review? Repurchase?

## Persona Template

```
## Persona: [Name] — [One-line description]

### Demographics
- Age: [range]
- Location: [country/region]
- Income: [range]
- Occupation: [title/industry]
- Life stage: [e.g., working parent, college student]

### Psychographics
- Values: [3-5 key values]
- Lifestyle: [brief description]
- Interests: [3-5]
- Shopping personality: [e.g., researcher, impulse buyer, deal hunter]

### Pain Points & Motivations
- Pain points: [what problems they face]
- Motivations: [what drives the purchase]
- Purchase triggers: [what sparks buying]
- Purchase barriers: [what holds them back]

### Media & Channel Reach
- Active on: [platforms with frequency]
- Content preference: [formats they engage with]
- Trusted sources: [influencer types, review sites, friends]
- Best channels to reach: [ranked 1-3 with rationale]

### Purchase Journey
- Awareness: [how they discover]
- Consideration: [how they evaluate]
- Decision: [what closes the sale]
- Post-purchase: [review, share, repurchase behavior]

### Messaging Angles
- Primary hook: [what resonates most]
- Supporting benefits: [2-3]
- Objection handling: [how to address top barriers]
- CTA style: [e.g., "Shop Now" vs. "Learn More" vs. limited-time offer]
```

## Audience Segmentation Models

### RFM Segmentation (for existing customers)
- **Recency**: How recently did they purchase?
- **Frequency**: How often do they purchase?
- **Monetary**: How much do they spend?

| Segment | R | F | M | Strategy |
|---------|---|---|---|----------|
| Champions | High | High | High | Loyalty rewards, early access, referrals |
| Loyal | High | Med | Med-High | Upsell, cross-sell, subscription |
| Potential Loyalist | High | Low-Med | Med | Nurture, second purchase incentive |
| New Customers | High | Low | Low | Welcome series, onboarding, education |
| At Risk | Low | Med-High | Med-High | Win-back campaigns, personalized offers |
| Hibernating | Low | Low | Low | Reactivation, low-cost offer, or suppress |

### Cohort Segmentation
- By acquisition channel (paid social vs. organic vs. referral)
- By acquisition campaign / creative
- By first product purchased
- By signup month / season
- By device (mobile vs. desktop)

### Psychographic Segmentation
- **Deal hunters**: Price-sensitive, coupon-driven, comparison shoppers
- **Brand loyalists**: Trust-driven, repeat purchasers, less price-sensitive
- **Quality seekers**: Research-heavy, review-dependent, willing to pay more
- **Convenience buyers**: Speed-focused, Amazon Prime, one-click, subscribe & save
- **Trend followers**: Early adopters, social-media-driven, aesthetic-focused
- **Ethical consumers**: Sustainability, fair trade, cruelty-free, brand values matter

## Persona Validation Checklist

- [ ] Demographics are specific (not "women 18-49" — narrow to meaningful bands)
- [ ] Pain points are concrete and product-relevant (not generic "wants quality")
- [ ] Purchase triggers are identifiable moments or events (not "needs product")
- [ ] Media consumption maps to actual reachable channels with content formats
- [ ] Messaging angles directly address the persona's top pain point and motivation
- [ ] At least 2-3 distinct personas (not one "everyone" persona)
- [ ] Personas are prioritized by revenue potential and reachability
- [ ] Each persona has a defined primary channel and secondary channel
- [ ] Persona assumptions are labeled as "verified" (from data) vs. "hypothesis" (needs testing)
