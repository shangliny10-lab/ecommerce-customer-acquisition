# Platform Acquisition Guides

## Amazon

### Traffic Sources Within Amazon
1. **Organic search ranking** (A9/A10 algorithm)
2. **Sponsored Products / Brands / Display** (PPC)
3. **Amazon's Choice / Best Seller badges**
4. **Category browsing** (left nav, filters)
5. **Frequently bought together** / **Customers also bought**
6. **Deal pages** (Lightning Deals, Best Deals, Prime Day)
7. **Amazon Posts** (social-style content within Amazon)
8. **Amazon Live** (live streaming)
9. **Brand Store / Storefront**
10. **Vine program** (early reviews)

### Off-Amazon Traffic (Amazon Attribution)
- **Amazon Attribution**: Track external traffic (Google, Meta, TikTok, email) to Amazon conversions
- **Social media**: Drive to Amazon product pages or storefront
- **Influencers**: Amazon Associates / Influencer Program (commission-based)
- **Google Ads**: Direct to Amazon (but consider margin — Amazon fees + ad cost)
- **Email**: Drive existing customers to Amazon for repeat purchases

### Key Optimization Levers
- **Title**: Brand + core keyword + key attributes + use case (under 200 chars)
- **Main image**: White background, 1000×1000px+, product fills 85% of frame
- **Secondary images**: Lifestyle, infographics, details, comparison, packaging
- **Bullet points**: 5 bullets, each starts with benefit in ALL CAPS, then explanation
- **Description / A+ Content**: Brand story, comparison charts, lifestyle imagery
- **Backend search terms**: 249 bytes, no repeats, no brand names, no punctuation
- **Reviews**: Quantity and quality (rating) heavily impact ranking and conversion
- **Pricing**: Competitive but profitable (Buy Box eligibility requires competitive pricing)
- **Inventory**: In-stock rate affects ranking (out of stock = ranking drop)

### Amazon SEO Ranking Factors (A10)
1. Sales velocity (recent sales weighted more heavily)
2. Conversion rate (sessions → purchases)
3. Click-through rate from search results
4. Price competitiveness (Buy Box)
5. Reviews (quantity, rating, recency, helpful votes)
6. Listing completeness (all fields filled, A+ content)
7. Inventory health (in-stock, FBA)
8. Account health (ODR, late shipment, policy compliance)
9. Off-Amazon traffic (Amazon Attribution — increasingly weighted)
10. Return rate (lower is better)

## Shopify / DTC Independent Store

### Traffic Acquisition Channels
1. **Paid social** (Meta, TikTok, Pinterest) — primary for new DTC brands
2. **Organic social** (TikTok, Instagram, Pinterest, YouTube)
3. **SEO / content marketing** (blog, guides, comparison pages)
4. **Google Ads** (Shopping, Search, Performance Max)
5. **Email marketing** (owned channel, highest ROI)
6. **Influencer / affiliate marketing**
7. **Marketplace sales** (Amazon, Etsy, Walmart — as additional channels)
8. **PR / brand mentions**
9. **Referral / loyalty programs**
10. **Community** (Facebook groups, Discord, Reddit)

### Shopify-Specific Tools
- **Shopify Email**: Built-in email marketing (free up to 10K emails/month)
- **Shopify Forms**: Email capture pop-ups
- **Shopify Audiences**: Customer list for ad platform lookalikes
- **Shopify Collabs**: Influencer collaboration management
- **Shop Pay**: Accelerated checkout (increases conversion by 10-15%)
- **Shopify Markets**: Multi-currency, multi-language, international domains
- **Apps**: Klaviyo (email), Loox/Yotpo (reviews), Recharge (subscriptions), Smile (loyalty)

### DTC vs. Marketplace Tradeoffs
| Factor | DTC (Shopify) | Marketplace (Amazon) |
|--------|---------------|---------------------|
| Customer data | Full ownership (email, behavior) | Limited (anonymized) |
| Profit margin | Higher (no 15% referral fee) | Lower (fees + FBA) |
| Traffic acquisition | Must drive all traffic yourself | Built-in audience, but competitive |
| Brand control | Full (site design, content, story) | Limited (within Amazon framework) |
| Trust barrier | Must build trust from scratch | Amazon trust built-in |
| Conversion rate | 1-3% (typical) | 10-15% (typical) |
| Scalability | Scales with marketing investment | Scales with ranking + PPC |
| Best for | Brand building, higher AOV, repeat purchases | Volume, reach, new product validation |

## TikTok Shop

### Traffic Sources
1. **For You Page (FYP)** — algorithmic discovery (organic)
2. **TikTok Ads** — In-Feed, Spark Ads, TopView
3. **TikTok Shop tab** — in-app shopping destination
4. **Product search** — in-app search (growing)
5. **Creator videos** — shoppable videos with product tags
6. **Live shopping** — live streams with exclusive deals
7. **Affiliate creators** — commission-based promotion
8. **Shop Showcase** — brand storefront within TikTok
9. **Hashtag challenges** — branded UGC campaigns
10. **Series / Playlists** — content collections

### TikTok Shop Seller Requirements
- **Eligible markets**: US, UK, Indonesia, Malaysia, Thailand, Vietnam, Philippines, Singapore, Mexico, Brazil (expanding)
- **Business license**: Required for business accounts
- **Product categories**: Some restricted (beauty, health, food require additional approval)
- **Fulfillment**: TikTok Shop Fulfilled (TTF) or seller-fulfilled
- **Performance metrics**: Order cancellation rate <2.5%, late shipment <4%, return rate monitored

### TikTok Shop Growth Strategy
1. **Product seeding**: Send products to 50-100 micro creators (organic UGC)
2. **Affiliate program**: Set competitive commission (10-20%) to attract creators
3. **Spark Ads**: Boost best-performing organic creator videos (proven creative)
4. **Live shopping**: Regular live streams (3-5/week) with demos and exclusive deals
5. **Shoppable videos**: Tag products in every organic video
6. **Creator marketplace**: Use TikTok Creator Marketplace to find and collaborate
7. **Hashtag strategy**: Branded hashtag + niche hashtags + trending hashtags
8. **Shop optimization**: Complete shop profile, high-quality product images, competitive pricing
9. **Reviews**: Encourage reviews (increases conversion and search ranking)
10. **Deals**: Participate in platform deals (Flash Sale, Voucher, Free Shipping)

## Shopee / Lazada (Southeast Asia)

### Shopee Traffic Sources
1. **Shopee search** (organic ranking)
2. **Shopee Ads** (Keyword Ads, Discovery Ads, Shop Ads)
3. **Flash Deals** (time-limited discounts)
4. **Shopee Live** (live streaming)
5. **Shopee Video** (short video content)
6. **Category browsing** / **Daily Discover**
7. **Shopee Mall** (premium brand badge)
8. **Vouchers & Coins** (promotional tools)
9. **Followers** (shop follower base)
10. **Affiliate / Shopee Ambassador**

### Lazada Traffic Sources
1. **Lazada search** (organic)
2. **Lazada Sponsored Solutions** (PPC)
3. **LazLive** (live commerce)
4. **Lazada Feed** (content discovery)
5. **Flash Sales / Vouchers**
6. **LazMall** (brand mall)
7. **Category navigation**
8. **Affiliate program** (LazAffiliate)
9. **Push notifications** (app)
10. **Games & engagement** (LazGames, collectibles)

### SEA Market-Specific Tips
- **Mobile-first**: 90%+ of traffic is mobile, optimize for small screens
- **COD (Cash on Delivery)**: Still popular in many SEA markets — offer if possible
- **Free shipping**: Critical conversion factor (use free shipping vouchers)
- **Vouchers**: Shoppers expect vouchers — always have active vouchers
- **Chat responsiveness**: Respond to Shopee/Lazada chat quickly (affects ranking)
- **Local language**: Use local language (Bahasa Indonesia, Thai, Vietnamese, etc.)
- **Local holidays**: 9.9, 10.10, 11.11, 12.12 sales are massive (prepare inventory and deals)
- **Low price point**: SEA consumers are price-sensitive — competitive pricing essential
- **Live commerce**: Very popular in SEA — regular live streams drive significant sales
- **Influencer/KOL**: Local creators (TikTok, Instagram) drive discovery and trust

## Walmart Marketplace

### Traffic Sources
1. **Walmart.com search** (organic)
2. **Walmart Sponsored Products / Brands** (PPC)
3. **Walmart+ membership** (free shipping, member deals)
4. **Category browsing** / **Best sellers**
5. **Walmart Deals** (rollback, clearance)

7. **Walmart App** (push notifications, app-only deals)
8. **Affiliate program** (Walmart Partners)
9. **Marketplace seller badges** (Pro Seller, Fast & Free)
10. **Off-site**: Google Shopping (Walmart listings often rank)

### Walmart Seller Requirements
- **Business registration**: US-based business (EIN required)
- **Product catalog**: Minimum SKU count (varies by category)
- **Fulfillment**: WFS (Walmart Fulfillment Services) or seller-fulfilled with fast shipping
- **Pricing**: Must be competitive (Walmart monitors price competitiveness)
- **Performance**: On-time shipping >95%, order cancellation <2%, seller rating >4.0
- **Customer service**: Response time <24 hours

## Etsy

### Traffic Sources
1. **Etsy search** (organic — Etsy's search algorithm)
2. **Etsy Ads** (Promoted Listings, Offsite Ads)
3. **Etsy homepage / category pages** (editorial picks)
4. **Etsy Picks / Editors' Picks** (curated collections)
5. **Shop sections / favorites** (repeat visitors)
6. **Etsy search filters** (price, color, location, shipping)
7. **Social media** (Pinterest is huge for Etsy — visual products)
8. **SEO from Google** (Etsy listings often rank in Google)
9. **Etsy newsletters** (featured products)
10. **Etsy community** (teams, forums)

### Etsy SEO Key Factors
1. **Title**: First 3-4 words most important (matches search query)
2. **Tags**: 13 tags per listing — use all, long-tail phrases
3. **Categories**: Specific category placement (affects browsing)
4. **Attributes**: Fill all relevant attributes (material, color, size, occasion)
5. **Photos**: First photo drives CTR (use mockups, lifestyle)
6. **Reviews**: Quantity and rating (social proof)
7. **Recency**: New listings get a temporary boost
8. **Sales & conversion**: Listings that convert rank higher
9. **Shop quality**: Complete shop, about section, policies
10. **Shipping**: Fast/Free shipping badge (Etsy prioritizes)

## Google Shopping / Merchant Center

### Setup Requirements
1. **Google Merchant Center account** (free)
2. **Product feed** (submitted via API, scheduled fetch, or manual upload)
3. **Website with checkout** (must meet Google requirements)
4. **Shipping settings** (accurate shipping costs and times)
5. **Tax settings** (US only — sales tax configuration)
6. **Product data quality**: GTIN, brand, MPN, high-quality images, accurate pricing
7. **Landing page quality**: Must match feed data, load fast, have secure checkout

### Feed Optimization
- **Title**: Brand + Product Type + Key Attributes + Color/Size (under 150 chars)
- **Description**: Keyword-rich, first 150 chars most important (under 5000 chars)
- **Image**: 800×800px minimum, white background for apparel, multiple angles
- **GTIN**: Global Trade Item Number (critical for matching and ranking)
- **Brand**: Brand name (required for most categories)
- **MPN**: Manufacturer Part Number
- **Product Type**: Google product category (specific)
- **Attributes**: Color, size, material, pattern, gender, age group
- **Sale price**: If on sale, include both price and sale_price

### Google Shopping Campaign Types
- **Standard Shopping**: Manual bidding, product group segmentation
- **Performance Max**: Automated across all Google inventory (Search, Display, YouTube, Gmail, Discover)
- **Smart Shopping**: Legacy (being migrated to Performance Max)
- **Local Inventory Ads**: For physical stores (show in-stock products nearby)
- **Showcase Shopping**: For brand/category awareness (less common)

## Cross-Platform Strategy Matrix

| Goal | Best Platforms | Tactics |
|------|---------------|---------|
| **Brand awareness** | TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Pinterest | Short video, influencer, trending content |
| **Product discovery** | TikTok Shop, Amazon, Pinterest, Instagram Shopping | Shoppable content, discovery ads, visual search |
| **High-intent purchase** | Amazon, Google Shopping, Google Search | PPC, product listing optimization, retargeting |
| **Repeat purchase / LTV** | Email, SMS, Shopify (DTC), loyalty programs | Post-purchase flows, subscriptions, exclusive offers |
| **Trust / social proof** | YouTube reviews, Instagram, TikTok UGC, Reddit | Influencer reviews, customer testimonials, community |
| **Low-cost traffic** | SEO content, Pinterest, organic TikTok, Reddit | Long-tail keywords, evergreen content, community engagement |
| **Fast scaling** | Meta Ads, TikTok Ads, Amazon PPC | Paid social, Spark Ads, keyword expansion |
| **International expansion** | Shopify Markets, Amazon Global, local marketplaces | Multi-currency, local language, local payment, local influencers |

## Platform Selection Decision Tree

```
What is your primary goal?
├── Brand building & long-term equity
│   ├── Visual product? → Instagram + Pinterest + TikTok
│   ├── Tech/gadget? → YouTube + Reddit + tech blogs
│   └── B2B/wholesale? → LinkedIn + content + email
│
├── Sales volume & fast growth
│   ├── US market? → Amazon (PPC + SEO) + Meta Ads
│   ├── SEA market? → Shopee/Lazada + TikTok Shop
│   ├── EU market? → Amazon EU + Google Shopping + Meta
│   └── DTC brand? → Shopify + Meta/TikTok Ads + email
│
├── Low budget / organic only
│   ├── Visual product? → Pinterest + TikTok organic + Instagram
│   ├── Problem/solution? → SEO content + YouTube + Reddit
│   └── Niche community? → Reddit + Facebook Groups + Discord
│
└── Existing customer base (retention)
    ├── DTC store? → Email (Klaviyo) + SMS + loyalty
    ├── Amazon? → Amazon posts + Amazon Live + Brand Store
    └── Marketplace? → Followers + vouchers + live streaming
```
