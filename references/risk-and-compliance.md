# Risk & Compliance

## Advertising Policy Compliance

### Platform-Specific Policy Risks

#### Meta (Facebook/Instagram) Ads
- **Prohibited content**: Adult products/services, alcohol (restricted), tobacco, drugs/weight loss, weapons, gambling, misleading claims, sensational content
- **Personal attributes**: Cannot explicitly reference personal characteristics (race, religion, health, financial status) in ad copy
- **Before/after images**: Restricted for health/weight/skin categories
- **Testimonials**: Must be genuine, cannot fabricate; must disclose if compensated
- **Landing page requirements**: Must match ad promise, cannot have misleading pop-ups, must have privacy policy
- **Cryptocurrency/financial**: Pre-approval required
- **Political/election**: Must go through authorization process

#### TikTok Ads
- **Prohibited**: Adult content, tobacco, drugs, weapons, gambling, misleading claims, counterfeit goods
- **Creative restrictions**: No flashing images, no shocking content, no excessive text overlay (20% rule less strict but still relevant)
- **Music rights**: Must use licensed music from TikTok library or original audio
- **Influencer content**: Must disclose paid partnership (use "Paid Partnership" label)
- **Health/wellness**: Restricted claims, cannot promise specific results
- **Financial services**: Pre-approval required

#### Google Ads
- **Prohibited**: Counterfeit goods, dangerous products, deceptive practices, inappropriate content
- **Restricted**: Adult content, alcohol, gambling, health/medical, financial services, political
- **Landing page**: Must load fast, be relevant, have clear navigation, privacy policy
- **Trademarks**: Cannot use competitor trademarks in ad copy (in most regions)
- **Misrepresentation**: Cannot make false claims about price, availability, or product features
- **Destination requirements**: No malware, no phishing, no auto-redirects

#### Amazon PPC
- **Prohibited**: Cannot direct off-Amazon in ad copy, cannot use competitor brand names in targeting (some exceptions), cannot make false claims
- **Creative approval**: Sponsored Brands headlines and images must meet guidelines
- **Product eligibility**: Some categories require approval before advertising
- **Review manipulation**: Cannot incentivize reviews (violates TOS, can lead to account ban)

### Common Ad Disapproval Reasons & Fixes

| Issue | Platform | Fix |
|-------|----------|-----|
| "Personal attributes" in copy | Meta | Rephrase to avoid direct reference (e.g., "suffering from acne" → "acne-prone skin") |
| Before/after image | Meta/TikTok | Use product-only images, or blur before image, or use text description |
| Misleading health claim | All | Remove specific result promises, use "may help", "supports", add disclaimer |
| Landing page not loading | Google/Meta | Fix page, ensure fast load, no malware, no broken links |
| Trademark infringement | Google/Amazon | Remove competitor brand from copy, use generic terms |
| Excessive text in image | Meta (legacy) | Reduce text to <20% of image area, or use text in caption |
| Unsafe supplement claim | All | Remove disease treatment claims, stick to structure/function claims, add disclaimer |
| Age-restricted product | All | Set proper age targeting (18+/21+), add age gate on landing page |

## Influencer Marketing Compliance

### FTC Disclosure Requirements (US)
- **Must disclose** material connection between influencer and brand (free product, payment, affiliate)
- **Disclosure must be**: Clear, conspicuous, unavoidable, in the same language as the endorsement
- **Acceptable hashtags**: #ad, #sponsored, #partner, #paidpartnership (at beginning of caption, not buried)
- **Unacceptable**: #sp (too ambiguous), #collab (ambiguous), disclosure only in "more" link, disclosure in story that disappears
- **Video content**: Verbal disclosure AND on-screen text (for TikTok/Reels/YouTube)
- **Product seeding**: Even if only free product (no payment), disclosure is required if post promotes product

### Platform-Specific Disclosure Tools
- **Instagram**: "Paid Partnership" label (must be enabled by brand)
- **TikTok**: "Paid Partnership" label, #ad hashtag
- **YouTube**: "Includes paid promotion" label, verbal disclosure in video
- **Facebook**: "Paid Partnership" label
- **General**: Branded content tools are preferred over hashtag-only disclosure

### Influencer Contract Essentials
- [ ] Scope of work (number of posts, platforms, content type, usage rights)
- [ ] Compensation (amount, product, affiliate commission, payment terms)
- [ ] Timeline (content approval deadline, posting date, exclusivity period)
- [ ] Content approval process (brand review before posting, revision rounds)
- [ ] Usage rights (how long brand can use content, where, whitelisting rights)
- [ ] Exclusivity (cannot promote competitors during X period)
- [ ] Disclosure requirements (FTC compliance, branded content tools)
- [ ] Performance metrics (minimum reach/engagement, if guaranteed)
- [ ] Termination clause (what happens if content underperforms or creator breaches)
- [ ] Confidentiality (cannot share brand strategy, pricing, unreleased products)
- [ ] Indemnification (creator warrants content is original, doesn't infringe)

## Data Privacy & Compliance

### GDPR (EU/EEA)
- **Consent**: Must obtain explicit, opt-in consent for cookies, email marketing, ad targeting
- **Cookie banner**: Must have accept/reject options (cannot have "accept all" only)
- **Data subject rights**: Access, rectification, erasure ("right to be forgotten"), data portability
- **Data processing**: Must have legal basis for processing (consent, contract, legitimate interest)
- **Breach notification**: Within 72 hours of becoming aware
- **Privacy policy**: Must be clear, accessible, explain what data is collected and why

### CCPA/CPRA (California)
- **Right to know**: What personal data is collected, used, shared
- **Right to delete**: Request deletion of personal data
- **Right to opt-out**: Sale/sharing of personal data (must have "Do Not Sell My Personal Information" link)
- **Non-discrimination**: Cannot charge more or provide lower quality for exercising rights
- **Minors**: Opt-in for sale of data for users <16 (parental consent for <13)

### Email Marketing Compliance (CAN-SPAM / GDPR / CASL)
- **CAN-SPAM (US)**:
  - Accurate "From" and "Reply-To"
  - No deceptive subject lines
  - Include physical mailing address
  - Clear unsubscribe mechanism (honor within 10 business days)
  - No requirement for opt-in consent (but best practice)
- **GDPR (EU)**:
  - Must have explicit opt-in consent (soft opt-in for existing customers only)
  - Clear identification of sender
  - Easy unsubscribe (one-click preferred)
  - Privacy policy link
- **CASL (Canada)**:
  - Express or implied consent required
  - Sender identification and contact info
  - Unsubscribe mechanism (honor within 10 business days)

### Retargeting & Ad Tracking Compliance
- **Cookie consent**: Must obtain consent before setting advertising cookies (GDPR/CCPA)
- **Pixel tracking**: Must disclose in privacy policy
- **Opt-out mechanisms**: Provide link to opt out of ad personalization (Network Advertising Initiative, Digital Advertising Alliance)
- **Data minimization**: Only collect data necessary for stated purpose
- **Data retention**: Don't keep user data longer than necessary
- **Children's privacy (COPPA)**: Don't target children under 13, don't collect their data

## Product Claim & Advertising Compliance

### Prohibited / Restricted Claims
- **Medical/health**: Cannot claim to cure, treat, prevent disease (unless FDA-approved drug)
- **Supplements**: Can only make structure/function claims ("supports joint health"), not disease claims; must include disclaimer
- **Weight loss**: Cannot promise specific weight loss amounts or timelines; cannot use before/after in restricted contexts
- **Skin care**: Cannot claim to "cure acne" or "reverse aging" (use "improves appearance", "reduces look of")
- **Eco-friendly/green**: Must have substantiation for "eco-friendly", "sustainable", "biodegradable", "carbon neutral" (FTC Green Guides)
- **Made in USA**: Must meet FTC standard (all or virtually all made in USA)
- **Endorsements/testimonials**: Must reflect typical results, or disclose "results not typical"
- **Price claims**: "Sale" price must be genuine (offered at regular price for reasonable period first)
- **Originality**: Cannot claim "patented" unless patent exists; cannot claim "award-winning" without actual award

### Claim Substantiation
- Every objective claim in advertising must have reasonable basis (FTC requirement)
- Keep documentation: test results, clinical studies, certificates, survey data
- "Reasonable basis" depends on claim type (health claims need higher standard)
- If challenged, burden is on advertiser to prove claim is true

## Intellectual Property Risks

### Trademarks
- **Do not use competitor brand names** in ad copy, meta tags, or landing page content (in most jurisdictions)
- **Do not use logos** of other brands without permission
- **Register your own trademark** to protect brand and enable brand protection tools
- **Amazon Brand Registry**: Required for many protection features (Project Zero, Transparency)
- **Keyword bidding on competitor terms**: Legal in US (comparative advertising), but check platform policies and local laws

### Copyrights
- **Do not use copyrighted images/videos** without license (use stock photo sites, create original content)
- **Music in ads/videos**: Must use licensed music (platform libraries, royalty-free, or obtain license)
- **User-generated content**: Must obtain permission from creator before using in ads (even if tagged)
- **Influencer content usage rights**: Must be explicitly granted in contract (specify duration, platforms, modifications)

### Patents
- **Do not infringe on product patents** (research before sourcing/selling)
- **Utility patents**: Protect product function/design
- **Design patents**: Protect product appearance (common in Amazon infringement cases)
- **Patent trolling**: Common in ecommerce — have process for responding to infringement claims

## Account Safety & Risk Management

### Ad Account Ban Prevention
- **Start slow**: New accounts should start with low budgets, increase gradually
- **Warm up**: Don't launch many campaigns at once on a new account
- **Follow policies**: Read and follow platform advertising policies strictly
- **Use business manager**: Meta Business Manager / TikTok Business Center (more stable than personal accounts)
- **Have backup**: Maintain backup ad accounts (in case primary is banned)
- **Appeal promptly**: If account is restricted, appeal immediately with detailed explanation
- **Avoid risky categories**: Adult, weight loss, supplements, CBD, gambling are high-risk for account bans

### Marketplace Account Safety (Amazon, etc.)
- **Follow TOS strictly**: No review manipulation, no off-platform diversion, no counterfeit goods
- **Maintain good metrics**: ODR (Order Defect Rate) <1%, late shipment <4%, cancellation <2.5%
- **Respond to A-to-Z claims** within 72 hours
- **Keep inventory accurate** to avoid cancellation
- **Use FBA** for Prime eligibility and better metrics
- **Brand Registry** for protection and enhanced content
- **Don't open multiple accounts** (unless legitimate business need, pre-approved by Amazon)

### Crisis Response Plan
1. **Ad account banned**: Appeal immediately, switch to backup account, pause spend, review policy violation
2. **Negative viral content**: Respond quickly and authentically, don't delete criticism (unless abusive), offer resolution
3. **Product safety issue**: Stop sales immediately, investigate, issue recall if necessary, communicate transparently
4. **Data breach**: Notify affected users within legal timeframe, fix vulnerability, report to authorities if required
5. **Influencer scandal**: Pause content, assess brand association, decide whether to continue or cut ties
6. **Competitor attack**: Document evidence, report to platform, consider legal action if defamation

## Compliance Checklist

### Before Launching Any Campaign
- [ ] Ad copy reviewed for prohibited claims (health, medical, weight loss, etc.)
- [ ] Creative assets are original or properly licensed (images, music, video)
- [ ] Landing page has privacy policy, clear navigation, matches ad promise
- [ ] Targeting complies with platform policies (no protected attributes in copy)
- [ ] Cookie consent / tracking compliance (GDPR/CCPA)
- [ ] Email list obtained with proper consent (opt-in where required)
- [ ] Influencer content includes proper disclosure (#ad, paid partnership label)
- [ ] Product claims have substantiation documentation
- [ ] No competitor trademarks in ad copy or landing page
- [ ] Budget and pacing set to avoid account flags (new accounts)
- [ ] Tracking pixels and UTM parameters properly configured
- [ ] Unsubscribe mechanism tested and working (email)
- [ ] Physical mailing address included in marketing emails
