# Ecommerce Customer Acquisition

## Scope

This skill covers two tightly connected work modes:

1. **Acquisition strategy and planning.** Build channel-specific traffic acquisition plans, audience targeting maps, keyword clusters, content calendars, ad campaign structures, KOL outreach lists, and budget allocation models for ecommerce stores.
2. **Acquisition diagnosis and optimization.** Diagnose why traffic is low or costly, reverse-engineer competitor traffic sources, audit existing acquisition funnels, identify bleeding points, and produce prioritized optimization actions with measurable KPIs.

Treat paid ads and organic traffic as one acquisition system. Do not split them into separate skills — a complete acquisition plan must coordinate paid, owned, and earned channels.

## Core Workflow

1. **Classify the acquisition task.** Identify whether the user needs traffic strategy, keyword/SEO, competitor traffic analysis, social media引流, paid ad setup/optimization, KOL/influencer outreach, content marketing plan, email/retargeting, audience persona building, funnel optimization, CAC/ROI analysis, or a combined full-channel acquisition plan.
2. **Extract store and product context.** Pull out platform(s), target country/market, language, product/category, brand, price range, SKU count, current traffic sources, monthly traffic/sessions, conversion rate, AOV (average order value), current ad spend, CAC, ROAS, rating/reviews, competitive position, and the user's acquisition goal (traffic volume / sales / brand awareness / lead gen).
3. **Choose evidence paths before planning.** Read `references/traffic-channel-map.md` for the full channel inventory and `references/platform-acquisition-guides.md` for platform-specific rules. Decide whether to use public data (SimilarWeb-style reasoning, search result analysis, social listening), seller center reports, ad platform data, competitor pages, user files, or industry benchmarks.
4. **Check platform policy and compliance boundaries.** Read `references/risk-and-compliance.md`. Platform ad policies, influencer disclosure rules, GDPR/CCPA data requirements, and prohibited content categories constrain what acquisition tactics are viable. Official platform policies beat generic growth-hack advice.
5. **Build the audience and persona map.** Read `references/audience-persona.md`. Define ICP (ideal customer profile), demographic layers, psychographic traits, pain points, purchase triggers, media consumption habits, device preference, and journey stage (awareness / consideration / decision / retention). Map each persona to the channels where they are actually reachable.
6. **Build the keyword and search-intent map.** Read `references/keyword-research.md`. Create brand keywords, category keywords, product keywords, problem/solution keywords, comparison keywords, long-tail question terms, and local-language variants. Separate informational, navigational, commercial, and transactional intent.
7. **Reverse-engineer competitors when available.** Read `references/competitor-traffic-analysis.md`. Extract 3-5 direct competitors across estimated traffic volume, traffic source breakdown (organic search / paid / social / referral / direct), top ranking keywords, ad creative patterns, social media presence and engagement, KOL collaborations, content themes, and promotion tactics. Use competitors for gap discovery, not as copy source.
8. **Diagnose root causes when optimizing existing acquisition.** Read `references/funnel-diagnosis.md`. Separate likely causes into traffic volume (top-of-funnel), click-through rate, landing page bounce, add-to-cart rate, checkout completion, repeat purchase, and channel-specific issues. Map each problem to the funnel stage it affects.
9. **Generate channel-ready acquisition plans.** Read `references/acquisition-output-templates.md` and `references/social-media-playbook.md`, `references/ad-strategy.md`, `references/content-marketing.md`. Produce the requested channel plan, keyword list, ad campaign structure, content calendar, KOL outreach template, audience targeting table, funnel optimization actions, and budget allocation model. Complete every requested field or mark it `blocked: missing evidence`.
10. **Localize for target market.** Adapt buyer language, local platform dominance (e.g., KakaoTalk in Korea, LINE in Japan, Zalo in Vietnam), local holidays and shopping events, payment preferences, cultural references, influencer ecosystem, and content tone for the target country/market.
11. **Run risk and handoff checks.** Read `references/risk-and-compliance.md`. Flag policy violations, prohibited ad claims, undisclosed influencer partnerships, data privacy issues, unrealistic traffic promises, unsupported ROI projections, brand/IP terms, and budget risks.
12. **Give validation steps and KPIs.** Recommend the smallest next verification: set up tracking pixels/UTM, run a low-budget ad test, publish 3-5 content pieces and monitor engagement, outreach 10 KOLs and track response rate, monitor keyword ranking movement, or compare before/after session and conversion metrics. Define success metrics for each channel.

## Mandatory Quality Gates

Run all applicable gates before presenting final plans or completed diagnosis.

### 1. Evidence And Claim Gate

- Create a claim ledger for traffic volume estimates, conversion rates, CAC, ROAS, CTR, engagement rates, audience size, keyword difficulty, competitor traffic share, and market size.
- For each claim, record its source and scope: platform, country, time period, data source (seller report / ad platform / public estimate / industry benchmark).
- Distinguish verified internal data from external estimates and industry benchmarks. Never present an estimate as exact data.
- Delete or qualify claims such as "guaranteed traffic," "viral potential," "zero-cost acquisition," or unsupported ROI projections unless direct evidence supports the exact wording.
- Treat competitor behavior as research signal, not proof of compliance or profitability. Do not turn competitor tactics into affirmative recommendations without platform policy check.
- Maintain a `verified`, `conditional`, and `blocked` claim whitelist. Final plans may use only `verified` claims as facts; place conditional claims in validation notes.

### 2. Channel And Platform Gate

- Distinguish platform-native traffic (within marketplace search/feed) from off-platform traffic (social, search engine, email, referral). They have different mechanics, costs, and attribution.
- Determine for each channel whether the user's product category is allowed, whether the platform supports the target country, and whether the user's account has the necessary permissions (e.g., Amazon Attribution, TikTok Ads Manager).
- Never recommend a channel without verifying it reaches the target audience in the target market. A channel popular in one country may be irrelevant in another.
- Do not merge ad platforms (Google Ads / Meta Ads / TikTok Ads / Amazon PPC) — they have different auction mechanics, targeting options, creative formats, and attribution windows. Build platform-specific plans.
- Build a channel matrix and mark unresolved platform access or policy restrictions.

### 3. Plan Completeness And Actionability Gate

- Check the user's requested deliverables one by one. For a full acquisition plan, include audience map, channel selection with rationale, keyword list, content plan, ad campaign structure, KOL strategy, budget allocation, timeline, KPI targets, and risk notes unless the user narrows scope.
- Every recommended action must be specific enough to execute: who does what, on which platform, with what content/creative, targeting whom, with what budget, and how success is measured. Vague actions like "improve SEO" or "post on social media" are not acceptable.
- Budget allocation must sum to the user's stated total budget (or explicitly state assumptions if budget is unknown).
- Timeline must have concrete phases (week 1-2 setup, week 3-4 test, month 2 scale, etc.) with milestones.
- Validate that recommended tactics are feasible for the user's resource level (team size, budget, technical capability). A plan requiring a 10-person content team is useless for a solo seller.

### 4. Funnel Consistency Gate

- Re-read the final plan, tables, and summary as one deliverable.
- Confirm that traffic sources map to funnel stages, that messaging matches the audience's journey stage, and that CTAs align with the desired action at each stage.
- Remove contradictory actions such as "focus on brand awareness" in one section and "optimize for immediate purchase" in another without explaining how they coexist.
- Ensure that top-of-funnel traffic has a corresponding middle- and bottom-of-funnel nurture path. Sending cold traffic directly to a product page without nurture is a common leak.
- Never claim that tracking, attribution, or ROI measurement is set up unless the plan explicitly includes the tracking setup steps.

### 5. Mixed-Task Boundary Gate

- Identify when the request also requires listing/product page optimization (handoff to `doubao-listing-localization`), inventory/replenishment, margin/forecast modeling, logistics, tax, or legal eligibility.
- Complete the acquisition-side work that is supported, then explicitly route each remaining workstream to the appropriate skill or agent. State that the overall task is not complete until those workstreams return.
- Do not improvise listing copy, product pricing decisions, inventory forecasts, or financial conclusions inside this skill and present them as validated business outputs.

## Input Rules

- Platform(s) and target country/market are the highest-value inputs. If missing and the answer depends on platform rules or local channel dominance, ask a concise question. If proceeding asynchronously, default to `Amazon US + Instagram/TikTok US` only when the query implies a US market; otherwise use `global ecommerce` and mark assumptions.
- Product/category and price range are required for audience targeting and channel selection. If missing, give an input checklist and template rather than inventing product details.
- Current traffic data (sessions, conversion rate, AOV, current CAC) materially changes the diagnosis. Ask for it when optimizing existing acquisition, but proceed with benchmark-based assumptions if the user cannot provide it.
- Budget is required for paid ad planning. If the user has not stated a budget, provide a scalable plan with budget tiers (e.g., $500/mo / $2000/mo / $10000/mo) rather than inventing a number.
- Treat user-provided seller center exports, ad platform reports, analytics screenshots, and competitor links as first-party input, but label unverified metrics and backend-only gaps.
- A competitor's visible social media presence does not prove their traffic volume or ROI. Label `visible presence`, `estimated reach`, and `actual performance (unknown)` separately.
- Public traffic estimate tools can support directional analysis, but they do not prove exact session counts, conversion rates, or revenue.
- Do not invent search volume, keyword difficulty scores, ad CPC, audience size, engagement rates, market size, or competitor traffic numbers. Use ranges with clear "estimated" labeling, or state that the data requires a paid tool to verify.

## Quality Bar

A good answer:

- Starts with task understanding, key assumptions, missing inputs, and the selected work mode.
- Names the platform(s)/market, product/category, and acquisition target.
- Uses official platform policy paths before making platform-specific recommendations.
- Outputs structured tables for audience persona, channel selection, keyword map, ad campaign structure, content calendar, KOL list, budget allocation, funnel diagnosis, and prioritized actions as needed.
- Separates final plan, rationale, validation gaps, and data-availability unknowns.
- Preserves channel/platform evidence scope and never applies a tactic that works on one platform to another without adaptation.
- Completes all requested acquisition fields and passes the evidence, channel, actionability, funnel, and final consistency gates.
- Adapts strategy to the target market's local platform ecosystem instead of applying a one-size-fits-all US-centric playbook.
- Flags policy, compliance, data privacy, brand/IP, and budget risks.
- Gives a concrete validation loop with KPIs, tracking setup, and expected timelines to see results.

Avoid:

- Generic "grow your traffic" advice without specific, executable actions.
- Fabricated traffic numbers, search volumes, CPC, ROAS, or market size.
- Recommending channels without verifying they reach the target audience in the target market.
- Treating competitor visible activity as proof of profitability or compliance.
- Ignoring platform ad policies or influencer disclosure regulations.
- Sending all traffic to the homepage or product page without funnel-stage-appropriate landing pages.
- Copying competitor ad creative or content as a fix.
- Promising guaranteed traffic, viral success, or specific ROI numbers.
- Solving listing optimization, pricing, inventory, logistics, or legal/tax questions inside this skill when they should be handed off.

## Default Output Sections

Use this structure unless the user asks for a different format:

1. **Task understanding and assumptions**
2. **Source / data verification plan**
3. **Store and product context extraction**
4. **Audience persona and targeting map**
5. **Keyword and search-intent map**
6. **Competitor traffic analysis (when available)**
7. **Channel selection and acquisition plan**
   - Organic search / SEO
   - Paid ads (platform-specific)
   - Social media
   - KOL / influencer
   - Content marketing
   - Email / retargeting
   - Other channels
8. **Funnel diagnosis and optimization (for existing stores)**
9. **Budget allocation and timeline**
10. **KPI targets and measurement plan**
11. **Localization, policy, and risk check**
12. **Next validation steps**
13. **Final quality-gate result**

For pure strategy planning, sections 4-7 and 9-10 may be the main output. For acquisition diagnosis, sections 3, 6, 8, and 12 are mandatory.

## Handoff Rules

- If the user needs product listing / title / bullet / description optimization to convert the traffic, hand off to `doubao-listing-localization`.
- If the user asks whether a product can legally be advertised, certified, imported, taxed, shipped, or avoids infringement, hand off to `doubao-ecommerce-compliance-tax-logistics`.
- If the user asks about FBA/FBM setup, overseas warehouse strategy, customs, HS codes, or shipping cost optimization, hand off to `doubao-ecommerce-compliance-tax-logistics`.
- If the user asks for detailed PPC bid management, ACOS/TACOS optimization at the keyword level, or ongoing ad account management beyond campaign structure, mark it as operational execution outside this skill's planning scope.
- If the user asks for TikTok scripts, UGC video briefs, creator content, or short-video material after acquisition positioning is clear, hand off to `doubao-cross-border-growth-content`.
- If the user needs product research, category selection, or market sizing to decide what to sell, hand off to `doubao-product-selection`.
- If one request combines acquisition work with listing optimization, inventory, procurement, finance, forecasting, or logistics, provide the acquisition deliverable and a workstream handoff table. Do not describe the integrated task as complete until the responsible skills or agent have validated their parts.
